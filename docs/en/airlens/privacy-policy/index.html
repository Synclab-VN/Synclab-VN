Privacy Policy – AirLens

Learn how AirLens handles camera, microphone, video, audio, and local network streaming data.

1. Introduction

AirLens is an Android application that allows users to stream live video and audio from their Android devices directly to OBS Studio over a local Wi-Fi network.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

2. Information Collection

AirLens does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to external servers.

The application does not require user registration or collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

3. Camera and Microphone Permissions

AirLens requires access to the following permissions:

  • Camera: To capture live video from your Android device.
  • Microphone: To capture audio when audio streaming is enabled.

Video and audio data are transmitted directly from your Android device to OBS Studio over your local Wi-Fi network.

This data is not uploaded to AirLens servers or shared with third-party services.

4. Data Sharing

AirLens does not sell, share, or disclose personal information to third parties.

The application does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking services.

5. Data Storage and Security

AirLens does not maintain external servers for collecting or storing user video or audio streams.

Streaming data is transmitted directly over the local network between the Android device and OBS Studio.

6. Children’s Privacy

AirLens is not specifically designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
